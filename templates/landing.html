How It Works

1

Submit Your Idea

Describe your startup idea in plain English. Optionally add URLs to articles, competitor sites, or research you've already found.

2

AI Research

Our Research Agent searches the web for market data, competitors, technical solutions, and relevant resources. You get a structured research brief.

3

Adversarial Debate

The Advocate argues why your idea will succeed. The Critic finds flaws and challenges assumptions. A Creative Agent suggests pivots and niches.

4

Judge's Verdict

The Judge weighs all arguments and gives you a scored verdict: PROCEED (build it), PARK (revisit later), or PRUNE (move on).

5

Your Decision

Review the verdict and debate transcript. Approve to generate a PRD, rebut with counter-arguments, or abort and try a different idea.

6

Complete PRD

Get a comprehensive Product Requirements Document with functional requirements, technical architecture, milestones, and acceptance criteria.
